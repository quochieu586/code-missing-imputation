\begin{frame}{Static Facts About Each Location}
Each location has static facts that may help the model predict variant fractions: continent, income classification, group, and median age.
\par\medskip
\begin{center}
\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\hline
Field & Values & Encoding & Output size \\\hline
Continent & 6 categories & Learned lookup table & 32 numbers \\
Income class & 4 categories & Learned lookup table & 32 numbers \\
Group & 4 categories & Learned lookup table & 32 numbers \\
Median age & 1 standardized number & Linear layer & 32 numbers \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\medskip
The model adds the four vectors to form $s$. It adds $s$ to every time step and part of the location.
\par\medskip
The model does not use location identity. A separate code for each location can memorize countries.
